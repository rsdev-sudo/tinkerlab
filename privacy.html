Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Tinkerlab (tinkerlab.fyi) is a small hobby site of games and tools. This page explains, in plain English, what information the site uses.

What we collect

We don't ask for accounts, names, emails or any personal details. Nothing you type into the games is sent to us.

Saved scores and progress

Some games save your best streak or the story endings you've found. This is stored only in your own browser (using "local storage") and never leaves your device. You can clear it any time through your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see anonymous, aggregate information such as which pages are visited and roughly what country visitors come from. It is designed to be privacy-friendly and does not build profiles of individual visitors.

Cookies and your choice

The site shows a cookie banner on your first visit. Your choice (accept or reject non-essential cookies) is saved in your browser, and you can change it any time using "Cookie settings" in the page footer. Rejecting has no effect on how the games work.

Advertising (future)

We may show ads in future using Google AdSense. If we do, Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and EU), we'll ask for your consent first, and you'll be able to change your choice at any time. This policy will be updated before ads are switched on.

Third parties

The site is hosted by Vercel. Their infrastructure processes basic technical data (such as your IP address) in order to deliver pages to you.

Error reports

If you use the "Report an error" button, your email app opens with a pre-filled message. We only receive what you choose to send, including your email address.

Accuracy

The games are for entertainment only, and facts used in them have not yet been independently verified.
