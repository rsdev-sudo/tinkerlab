Sources & Notes

The clues in this game are written from general geography knowledge — the kind of fact you'd find repeated across sources like the CIA World Factbook, National Geographic, or Wikipedia. They have not yet been individually fact-checked or cited against a specific source, so treat them as "probably true and fun," not as a verified reference.

We're planning to go back through each clue and confirm it against a reliable source properly. If you spot something that seems wrong in the meantime, we'd genuinely like to know.
