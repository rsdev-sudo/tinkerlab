About Tinkerlab

Tinkerlab is a small, independent collection of free browser games, quizzes and tools: geography and sports guessing games, higher-or-lower challenges, puzzles, a daily map game and a text adventure. No accounts, no downloads, and it works on your phone.

Why it exists

It started as a hobby project: a place to build the kind of quick, curious games that are fun for five minutes at a time. New games are added regularly.

Where the facts come from

Game data comes from public sources such as Wikipedia, open datasets and official records. It is being checked game by game, and mistakes can still slip through. Every game has a "Report an error" button, and we correct things when you tell us.

Your data

Scores and streaks are saved only in your own browser. Read the privacy policy for the full details, or change your cookie settings.
